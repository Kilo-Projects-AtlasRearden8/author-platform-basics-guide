# 5. ARC readers

ARC readers (Advance Reader Copy readers) read your book before it publishes and leave honest reviews around launch day. They are volunteers, not paid promoters.

## How ARC readers help

- Early reviews on launch day help new readers decide
- Feedback on typos or confusing parts before publication
- Word-of-mouth in their own communities

## Step 1: Decide what you are offering

- The finished ebook (PDF or EPUB), free
- A copy of the published book after launch (optional thank-you gift)
- A mention in the book's acknowledgments (ask before naming anyone)

You do not need to send physical copies. Ebooks are free to distribute.

## Step 2: Recruit for free

Ask people who already want to hear from you:

- Your newsletter subscribers
- Your social followers
- Library or bookstore contacts from your local events
- Topic-related online communities (where self-promotion is allowed)

Post something like:

> I'm looking for **[number]** ARC readers for **[Book title]**, a **[genre]** book about **[topic]**, launching **[date]**. You get a free advance copy and I ask for an honest review (good or bad) around launch day. Reply to this post or email **[author email]** if you're interested.

## Step 3: Send the copy (privacy-safe)

- Send by email or a file-sharing link.
- Use BCC if emailing multiple readers at once so nobody sees anyone else's address.
- Do not require readers to share personal data beyond an email address.
- Watermarking is optional; do not collect IDs or addresses.

## Step 4: Set expectations clearly

Tell ARC readers:

- When the book launches
- Where you'd like reviews (Amazon, Goodreads, StoryGraph — reader's choice)
- That an honest review is fine (a critical review is acceptable)
- The date by which you'd appreciate reviews
- That there is no obligation to review

## Step 5: FTC disclosure (United States)

If you give someone a free copy in exchange for a review, the reviewer should disclose that they received a complimentary copy. You cannot control what they write, but you can suggest language:

> I received a free advance copy of this book from the author. This is my honest review.

Include this suggestion in your ARC reader welcome email. It protects them and you.

## Privacy-safe checklist

- [ ] No payment for reviews (that is against retailer policy and undermines trust)
- [ ] No pressure for positive reviews — honest only
- [ ] BCC group emails so reader addresses stay private
- [ ] Do not share the ARC reader list with anyone
- [ ] Delete the list after launch if you do not need it anymore
