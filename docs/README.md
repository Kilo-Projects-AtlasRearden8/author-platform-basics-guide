# Author Platform Basics — Beginner Manual

Welcome. This manual walks you through building an author platform for free, with no ad budget, in a privacy-safe way. It is written for beginners: plain English, step by step, never condescending.

You do not need a marketing degree, a mailing list, or money for ads. You need a book (or one coming soon), an hour or two a week, and the willingness to show up where your readers already are.

## The mindset: platform before promotion

An author platform is not an ad campaign. It is the collection of places where readers can find you and hear from you directly:

- Your bio (on retailer pages, your site, event flyers)
- Your newsletter list (people who asked to hear from you)
- Your social accounts (where readers already hang out)
- Your ARC readers and reviewers (early supporters)
- Local events (libraries, bookstores, community rooms)

Start with one chapter. Finish it before moving to the next. A small platform you actually use beats a big one you abandon.

## Privacy ground rules (used throughout)

- Do not put your home address, personal phone number, or private email in public templates. Use placeholders like `[your-email@example.com]` or a dedicated author email.
- Never buy an email list. Never scrape readers' contact info. Only email people who opted in.
- Use double opt-in for newsletters (subscribers confirm their email).
- Keep any sign-in sheet at a local event minimal: name and email only, and say how you will use it.
