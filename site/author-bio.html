1. Author bio

Your author bio answers one reader question: Why should I listen to this person? You need it for:

  • Amazon / KDP author page
  • Goodreads / StoryGraph author profile
  • The back cover of your book
  • Your website or link-in-bio page
  • Event flyers and library introductions

Prepare three lengths: short (25–50 words), medium (75–125 words), and long (150–250 words). Write in the third person ("Dylan Asdale is..."), even though you are writing about yourself.

Step by step

Step 1: Gather your facts

Write down:

  • Your name (or pen name)
  • What you write (genre, topics, format)
  • One relevant credential or life detail (career, training, lived experience)
  • Where you are based (state level only, unless you want to share more)
  • One personal, human detail (hobby, family, pet — optional but warm)
  • Where readers can find you (website, newsletter)

Step 2: Draft the medium version first

Fill in this template:

[Name] is a [role/credential] who writes [genre/topic] for [audience]. [One sentence about background or why this topic matters]. [Name] lives in [state] with [family/pet/hobby]. [Book title] is [one-line description]. Readers can sign up for [Name]'s newsletter at [website].

Step 3: Trim it down (short) and expand it (long)

  • Short: keep name, what you write, one credential, and where to find you.
  • Long: add the backstory, the "why I wrote this book" moment, and a line about what readers say.

Example (for illustration only — replace with your own)

Dylan Asdale is a registered nurse and founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC, an Indiana publisher of beginner-friendly AI education. He writes practical guides that explain AI "like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending. His book The Massive AI Manual helps non-technical readers use AI tools confidently. Dylan lives in Indiana. Find his free guides and newsletter at dylanasdale.github.io.

Template — copy and fill in the bracketed parts.
[Full name] is a [role/credential] based in [state/region]. They write [genre/topic] for [audience]. [Brief background or why you wrote this book]. [Full name] lives in [state/region] with [optional personal detail]. [Book title] is [one-sentence description]. Readers can find more at [website].

Privacy-safe checklist

  • No home address (state or city only, or nothing at all)
  • No personal phone number
  • No private email — use an author email or contact form
  • No family member names unless they are public figures or have agreed
