How to use this guide

  1. Read the chapters in order, or jump to the topic you need now.
  2. Copy a template (Markdown versions are in docs/templates/) and fill in your book details.
  3. Follow the privacy-safe checklist at the end of each chapter.
  4. Work one chapter at a time. A small platform you actually use beats a big one you abandon.

Privacy by design

  • No home addresses, personal phones, or private emails in any template — use placeholders like [your-email@example.com].
  • No buying email lists or scraping reader data.
  • Newsletter setup uses double opt-in and explains data-minimization habits.
  • Local-event sign-in sheets collect only name and email, with a clear privacy note.
