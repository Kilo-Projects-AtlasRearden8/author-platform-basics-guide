6. Reviews

Reviews help readers decide whether to buy. This chapter is about earning honest reviews ethically — never buying them, never trading "I review yours if you review mine," and never pressuring readers.

Where reviews matter

  • Amazon — the biggest retailer; reviews appear on the book page
  • Goodreads — readers discover books here
  • StoryGraph — growing alternative to Goodreads
  • Library catalogs and book blogs — good for local visibility

How to ask (ethically)

Ask after someone has read the book, not before.

Good ways to ask:

  • A short note at the back of your book: "If you enjoyed this, a quick review on Amazon or Goodreads helps other readers find it."
  • Your newsletter: mention you'd appreciate honest reviews around launch.
  • Social posts: thank reviewers publicly (only where they already posted publicly).

Never say "leave a 5-star review" or offer anything in exchange for a review. Honest means honest — critical reviews are allowed.

Who not to ask

  • Friends and family reviewing on Amazon can trigger policy flags (Amazon discourages reviews from people with a personal relationship to the author). It is safer to ask them to share word-of-mouth instead.
  • Do not review-swap with other authors.
  • Do not pay for reviews.

Responding to reviews

  • Thank reviewers publicly only where they posted publicly (Goodreads, a blog, social).
  • Do not respond to Amazon reviews (you generally cannot, and shouldn't try).
  • Never argue with a critical review. A professional silence is the best response.

Reading your reviews

Look for patterns, not individual opinions. If several readers say the same thing ("chapter 3 was confusing," "I loved the examples"), that is useful feedback for the next edition or book.

Privacy-safe checklist

  • Never pay for reviews
  • Never offer gifts, discounts, or free books contingent on a positive review
  • Never review-swap with other authors
  • Do not pressure friends or family to post retailer reviews
  • Do not post a reviewer's private info anywhere
