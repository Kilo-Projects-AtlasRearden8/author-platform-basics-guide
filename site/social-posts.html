4. Social posts

Social media is where readers already hang out. You do not need to be everywhere. Pick one or two platforms your readers use, show up consistently, and repurpose what you already wrote.

Where to start (pick 1–2)

  • Bluesky / Mastodon — writer communities, good for conversation
  • X (Twitter) — readers, journalists, and book reviewers gather here
  • Instagram / Threads — visual, good for book covers and events
  • TikTok — book recommendations (#BookTok), requires video comfort
  • Facebook — local community groups and library pages still work well for local events

What to post (templates)

Templates — copy and fill in the bracketed parts.
NEW BOOK ANNOUNCEMENT My book [Book title] is out now! It's a [genre] book about [one-line description], written for [audience]. Available at [retailer links]. #[genre] #[topic] BEHIND THE SCENES Working on [chapter/section] today. This one took [number] tries to explain in plain English. That's the job. #amwriting #[topic] NEWSLETTER REMINDER New newsletter went out: [topic]. Join at [landing page link]. No ads, ever, and you can leave anytime. EVENT INVITE I'll be at [library/bookstore] on [date] at [time] talking about [topic]. Free and open to the public. Hope to see you there. REVIEW THANK-YOU (only where the reviewer posted publicly) Thank you [Reviewer name] for the kind review of [Book title] — it means a lot.

Posting cadence

  • 2–3 times per week is enough to start.
  • Same days each week helps readers know what to expect.
  • Batch-write: spend 30 minutes once a week drafting posts, then schedule or paste them in.

Repurpose without extra work

  • A newsletter issue → 3 social posts (announcement, tip, reminder)
  • A press release → 1 announcement post
  • A local event → 3 posts (invite, reminder, thank-you after)

Privacy-safe checklist

  • No home address, personal phone, or private email in posts
  • Do not post photos that show your home, street, or workplace without checking the background
  • Do not share readers' names or photos without permission
  • Keep author and personal accounts separate if you want to keep them separate
